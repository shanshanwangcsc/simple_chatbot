1. Q: What is soft-positive sampling?
  A: The positive pair for one audio sample is not from the exact corresponding video, but from a video of the same class.
2. Q: Which dataset used in the article?
   A: VGGsound, TAU Urban Audio-Visual Scenes 2021, and FSDnoisy18k
3. Q: How many classes does VGGSOUND have?
   A: 309
